Job offer response scripts: what to say first by phone or email, and what to check (free)

Got a job offer? This page has word-for-word scripts for your first reply, by phone or by email, a checklist of what to review, and a way to ask for more time. Fill in the brackets with your own details.

Three steps, in this order: thank them, get it in writing, ask for time. Don't accept or counter on the spot, even if the number is good. Asking for a day or two to decide is a reasonable request.

If the offer comes by phone

Thank you, I'm really glad to hear that. I'm excited about [the team / the work on X]. Could you send the full offer in writing, including [base salary, bonus, benefits, start date]? I'd like to review it carefully and get back to you by [day, usually 1-3 business days from now].

If they ask for a reaction right away:

I'm very interested, and I want to give it proper thought. I'll come back to you by [day] with any questions.

If the offer comes by email

Subject: Re: Offer for [job title]

Hi [name],

Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about the chance to join [company] and work on [specific thing].

I'm reviewing the details now and will get back to you by [day]. In the meantime, could you confirm [any missing details: the bonus structure, health benefits start date, remote-work policy, equity terms]?

Best,
[your name]

While you review: a checklist

  • Base salary, and how often it's paid
  • Bonus: guaranteed or discretionary? When paid?
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Health benefits, and when they start
  • Retirement plan or pension, and any employer match
  • Remote, hybrid or office days
  • Start date
  • Title and level
  • Anything that's only spoken, not written. Ask for it in writing.

Compare the whole package against the numbers you set for yourself: a target, a good outcome and a walk-away point. Then pick one or two things to ask for.

If you need more time than they gave you

Thank you again for the offer. I'm taking this decision seriously. Would it be possible to have until [new day] to finalize? I'm happy to answer any questions in the meantime.

Ask once. If they say no, decide within their deadline.

What this page isn't

It's help with what to say, not legal, tax or financial advice, or help with an employment dispute. No script can promise a higher offer, and some employers have fixed pay bands.
