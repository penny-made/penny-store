## Free resource: remote job sites that moved or closed, and the FTC's job-scam red flags

Lists of remote job sites go out of date: sites close, move to new addresses or stop posting, and older lists don't always catch up. On Oct 7, 2026, I opened every site on my list of remote job boards and hiring sites for a paid directory. This section is the free part of that check. The same text is also a page in the store: [Remote job sites that moved, closed or didn't load](https://ghillmann.gumroad.com/remote-job-sites-moved-or-closed).

### Moved or closed (as of Oct 7, 2026)

- **Stack Overflow Jobs:** closed on March 31, 2022. Some popular lists still include it.
- **JobsCollider:** its link now forwards to Remote First Jobs (remotefirstjobs.com).
- **Hubstaff Talent:** talent.hubstaff.com now forwards to hubstafftalent.net.
- **Older addresses:** remoteok.io → remoteok.com; remotive.io → remotive.com; angel.co → Wellfound.

### Couldn't check on Oct 7, 2026

- **Couldn't confirm activity:** Jobspresso and Authentic Jobs (the sites loaded, but my checker couldn't see listing dates) and Idealist (no listings visible to my checker).
- **Didn't load for my checker:** Remote.co and FlexJobs (timed out), Pangian (failed), HigherEdJobs (no listings shown), Outsourcely (the address didn't resolve) and ProZ.com (blocked automated visits).

Not loading for my checker can mean a site blocks automated visits, not that it's closed. Try them yourself.

### Job-scam red flags from the FTC

Quoted from the US Federal Trade Commission (quotes checked against its pages on Oct 7, 2026):

> “Never pay anyone to get paid, or to get a job. That's a sure sign of a scam.”

> “Ignore generic and unexpected texts or WhatsApp messages about jobs.”

Both from the FTC consumer alert “Task scams create the illusion of making money”, December 12, 2024: [consumer.ftc.gov/node/86820](https://consumer.ftc.gov/node/86820)

> “If someone offers you a job and claims that you can make a lot of money in a short period of time with little work, that's almost certainly a scam.”

> “Honest employers, including the federal government, will never ask you to pay to get a job.”

Both from the FTC “Job Scams” page (dated March 2023): [consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams)

If you're in the US and think you've seen a job scam, you can report it to the FTC at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov).

## Free: what 23 non-bedside nursing job postings had in common (checked Oct 7, 2026)

On Oct 7, 2026, I read 23 job postings for nurses outside hospital bedside roles. The employers were mostly health-tech companies, health plans and outpatient care groups, plus a drug-company support firm, a documentation consulting firm, a nursing school and a publisher. Big hospital systems weren't in the sample. That's a small sample from one day, so use it to learn what to look for, then check postings where you live. A longer version, with a 4-step way to check a role, is a free page in the store: [Non-bedside nursing jobs: what 23 real job postings had in common](https://ghillmann.gumroad.com/non-bedside-nursing-job-postings).

The first three points are about the 11 postings for utilization review, case management, triage and medical review.

- **A license, and which one.** All 11 asked for an RN license (two also accepted a social-work credential instead). Several wanted a license from one specific state (California in four, Michigan in one). One required a multistate (compact) license, and others accepted or preferred one. Read the license line first.
- **Certifications were rarely the gate.** None of the 11 required a certification. Several listed one as preferred (for example CCM).
- **Experience in the specialty was the gate.** 9 of the 11 required utilization review or management, case management, care management or care coordination experience already, from one year up to “3-5+ years”.
- **Two outpatient postings said experience wasn't required.** An oncology clinic said two or more years as an RN was “preferred but not required”, and a recruiter's ad for a clinic said previous experience was “ideal but not required”. An infusion center asked for hands-on skills instead (strong IV and infusion skills, including experience with specialty biologics) and named no number of years.

The [Nurse Career Change Kit](https://ghillmann.gumroad.com/l/nurse-career-change-kit) ($9) goes role by role through what the postings asked for, and adds 13 translations of bedside work into the words other roles use, resume summary examples, answers to “Why are you leaving the bedside?” and 7 AI prompts. It isn't medical, legal or licensing advice.
